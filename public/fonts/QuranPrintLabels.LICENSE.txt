Quran Print Labels (family "Quran Print Labels", version 1.000, CFF OpenType, 1000 units per em)
File: QuranPrintLabels.woff2 (111,228 B, sha256 67062851e94137deb06a6adfa893e72842565e018993f21eeaa9c900d0991ab7): the
generator's QuranPrintLabels.otf (1,235,388 B, sha256 6b86a924466abf981a55130e10b1683b50c6da41c77f6f460af57ff533790cbc)
in a WOFF2 container, the same tables losslessly compressed.

WHAT IT IS

The page furniture of the 1441H Madinah Mushaf print of the King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex, as glyph
outlines: the running heads «سُورَةُ name» (U+F600 + surah, 2 to 114) and «الجُزۡءُ …» (U+F700 + juz, 1 to 30), the
stacked margin labels of the 240 rubs (U+F800 + rub), the label words رُبُع، نِصۡف، ثَلَاثَةُ أَرۡبَاعِ، الحِزۡبُ،
الجُزۡءُ، سُورَةُ (U+F500 to U+F505) and the print's Eastern Arabic digits (U+0660 to U+0669). It holds no Quranic text.

PROVENANCE

The outlines are cut, not redrawn, from 123 pages of the Mushaf Database "Ligature-Based SVG Mushaf", version 1.01
(https://github.com/mushafdatabase/MushafDatabase-Ligature-Based-SVG, folder "SVG V1.01"; https://mushafdatabase.com/),
by assets/print-labels/build_print_labels.py in the Quran for Desktop repository; assets/print-labels/pages.txt lists
the pages and assets/print-labels/README.md tells how to regenerate the file. 64 margin labels are the print's own; the
other 176, whose pages were not among the inputs, are set from the words and digits cut from the print, with the
layout measured on the printed labels. The generator is deterministic and reproduces both files byte for byte.

The dataset's artwork derives from the printing-use Madinah Mushaf of the King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex
(KFGQPC), whose files KFGQPC allows to be used free of charge in computer programs
(https://dm.qurancomplex.gov.sa/rights/). The calligraphy remains the work of KFGQPC.
